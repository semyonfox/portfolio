Fox Focus combines tasks and calendar context in one planning view. Google Tasks owns the connected task content; approved actions can create tasks or update their status. The app has conflict checks and recovery for interrupted updates.

It runs as a personal deployment, not a multi-user service. Microsoft integrations are unconfigured or read-only.
