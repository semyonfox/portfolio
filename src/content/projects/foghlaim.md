Foghlaim saves lesson progress, grades exercises on the server and schedules vocabulary practice. Its searchable dictionary comes from reproducible imports of licensed Wiktionary data, with provenance and attribution retained.

It is a development preview. Billing runs in test mode only, and the definitions come from Wiktionary rather than being written for the app.
