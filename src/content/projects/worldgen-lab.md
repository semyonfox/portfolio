Given a seed, the current generator places a fixed entrance-to-exit corridor with one shop and adds seeded side rooms. It reports impossible target sizes and checks reachability. The same seed produces the same room layout.

This is a 2D vertical slice. General quotas, regions, navigation, enemies and 3D art are still planned work.
