I recorded a 64-second spacecraft flight path in Unity and used those poses to render paired Unity and Blender versions. A later 84-second Blender cut adds a cloak reveal, combat effects, hull fragments and a warp departure.

The separate MarsPhysics scene uses a Rigidbody with inverse-square central acceleration, tangential initial velocity and axial spin. The film choreography is authored animation, not a physics simulation.

This is a Star Trek fan scene. The ship models and several effects are third-party assets with credits in the project. The inherited planet texture still needs a confirmed source before the finished film is published here.
